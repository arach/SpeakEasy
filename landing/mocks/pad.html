WHAT YOU NEED

THE PAD is an iPadOS app, and it does not work alone. It needs SpeakEasy and Codex Desktop running on an Apple silicon Mac. That Mac holds the Parakeet model, the narration voices, and the bridge the nine lanes bind to.

Put both on the same Wi-Fi and choose your Mac in the app. Complete the connection and permission steps shown in Settings → Deck.

INTEGRATION BOUNDARY — THE CODEX BRIDGE USES PRIVATE, VERSION-CHECKED CODEX DESKTOP IPC AND FAILS CLOSED ON INCOMPATIBILITY. IT IS NOT PRESENTED AS A SUPPORTED PUBLIC CODEX API. SPEECH IS TRANSCRIBED BY THE PAIRED MAC; NO ARACH SPEECH SERVER SITS IN THE PATH.
