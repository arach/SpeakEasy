Open SpeakEasy → Settings → Deck on the Mac and scan the QR code shown there. The key travels in the link, so a copied address without it will not connect.

  1. Install the certificate

    The deck is served over HTTPS by a certificate authority running on your Mac. iOS needs a copy before it will trust it.

    Download the certificate

    Safari saves a profile, then asks you to review it in Settings.

  2. Approve it — two places

    Install: Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → tap the downloaded profile → Install.

    Then trust: Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings → turn on full trust for Caddy Local Authority.

    Both are required. Installing without enabling full trust is the usual reason the next step keeps failing.

    Checking trust…

  3. Open the deck

    Once trust is on, this opens the deck over HTTPS — which is also what lets the microphone work. Then Share → Add to Home Screen.

Why not just use HTTP? The deck loads fine over plain HTTP, but Safari only grants microphone access in a secure context. Without the certificate you get the surface without dictation.
