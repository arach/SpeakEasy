# A few visual notes from SpeakEasy

Hey—here are a few things that worked especially well for me in the native
SpeakEasy iPad and iPhone work:

- fine, carefully spaced typography
- crisp hairlines and control borders
- inactive states that stay structurally complete
- restrained, semantic color
- tonal surface separation with very little blur or shadow
- consistent spacing, radii, and numeric alignment

I put the short note and three native screenshots here:

`/Users/arach/dev/SpeakEasy/docs/design/speakeasy-visual-notes.md`

That is the whole frame. This is not a shared design system, a naming exercise,
or a request to bring SpeakEasy's metaphor or component structure into another
product. It is just a visual observation that may or may not be useful.

If something here resonates for your product, I would be interested in a very
short reaction—at most three bullets on what feels useful, what would not fit,
or one local wrinkle. No implementation is requested, and no reply is required.
